Retrain the ear with the music you grew up with

Pitch is one of the hardest things to hear through a hearing aid or cochlear implant. Short, regular listening practice can help. These exercises use the swaras of Shankarabharanam on a sruti you choose. Practise 10 to 15 minutes a day at a comfortable volume.
